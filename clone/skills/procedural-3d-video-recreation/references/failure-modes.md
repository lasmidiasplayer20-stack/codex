# Generalized failure prevention and regression guide

This guide converts the user's requested corrections into reusable checks for any reference video. Examples identify a failure class, not a required scene, character design, action, or fixed measurement. A new production must derive dimensions, timing, contacts, and camera from its own reference.

No finite checklist covers every possible defect. Treat this as a living regression catalog: add each newly reported or observed failure, preserve its reproducer, and generalize the prevention rule. Do not claim this catalog or its examples automatically validate a new film.

## Corrections and requirements carried forward from the original request

| ID | General failure class | Detect | Correct | Verify before marking resolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Inconsistent proportions between characters, props, and environments | Compare dimension ratios, silhouettes, and framing against the current source, including wide shots. | Establish a common unit scale and per-asset dimensions; fix asset or root scale rather than stretching bones. | Measure selected ratios and inspect equivalent source/reconstruction frames; confirm the fix in every reused scene. |
| R02 | Body intersects a chair, desk, floor, wall, vehicle, or other object | Measure signed clearance between oriented collision proxies, including mesh thickness, and review close contacts. | Adjust root pose, placement, joint targets, or collision response in the shared coordinate frame. | Sample the entire contact interval and intermediate transitions, not only its endpoints. No unintended penetration at the chosen tolerance. |
| R03 | Sitting floats above or sinks into a support | Compare seat top, hip volume, foot reach, and backrest clearance. | Derive pelvis placement from actual support height and hip bounds; calibrate the pose for that actor's scale. | Check seated hip clearance and feet/supports at all seated actions, scales, and orientations used in the film. |
| R04 | Furniture and actor face different directions, causing leg/support collisions | Transform actor, seat, tabletop, and supports to a common local frame. | Align placement/orientation and move supports or contact targets coherently. | Check feet and knees against actual oriented supports, including oblique camera views. Do not apply one scene's fixed offset globally. |
| R05 | Arms or legs stretch, snap, fold backward, or disconnect | Measure segment lengths, joint continuity, reach, and bend direction across time. | Use constant-length articulated limbs, valid IK poles and reach limits; reposition the body if a target is unreachable. | Test representative extremes and transition samples; distinguish clamped targets from successful contact. |
| R06 | Walking feet slide, float, penetrate the floor, or drift while planted | Track planted feet in world space against the actual root trajectory and terrain. | Couple stance/recovery to root speed, direction, and ground contact; lift the recovery foot. | Measure support-phase drift and floor clearance through speed changes, turns, and stops present in the source. |
| R07 | Lying down or standing up intersects the support or jumps across a cut | Review torso/head/limb bounds during the entire transition and compare poses immediately around cuts. | Use one continuous action phase across camera cuts; clear the edge before lowering limbs; maintain torso length. | Check support clearance and temporal continuity on both sides of every relevant cut. Generalize to any support or action transition. |
| R08 | Hands miss an object; objects jump, float, or pass through hands/furniture | Compare hand/contact transforms and object attachment before, at, and after interaction. | Use world-space contact targets and explicit attach/release events with continuous transforms. | Measure contact error and inspect the full interaction; unreachable IK cannot count as successful contact. |
| R09 | Object appears twice, ghosts, remains visible in the wrong shot, or has the wrong scale | Inspect the active scene graph, visibility state, identifiers, and composited frame. | Use explicit boolean visibility, one intended active instance, and calibrated dimensions; clear stale state at cuts. | Render times before/during/after appearance and revisit shots in a different evaluation order. Confirm expected object counts. |
| R10 | Character/object physics looks inconsistent | Check ground support, gravity direction, acceleration, impacts, rest states, and constraints against the intended stylization. | Use a suitable solver or coherent authored trajectories and contact constraints; avoid interpenetration and discontinuous velocity. | Review impacts and recovery in motion and frame by frame. Report whether behavior is simulated or directed. |
| R11 | Clothing collapses, intersects the body, changes volume, or has implausible folds | Inspect silhouettes, skinning, extreme poses, and body/cloth clearance. | Calibrate weights and joint deformation; use corrective shapes or validated cloth simulation where required. | Review bending, seated, raised-arm, and interaction poses that occur in the source. Rigid clothing must be identified as such; it does not satisfy a request for simulated folds. |
| R12 | Motion, camera, cuts, or scene content diverges from the source without disclosure | Compare the complete source timeline and matched-time samples, action landmarks, camera direction, and event order. | Revise the shot timeline and animation curves; use style references only for appearance. | Review every shot and important transition. Declare residual approximations; technical rig tests do not establish an exact clone. |
| R13 | Audio, music, or effects are absent, shifted, duplicated, or misrepresented | Inspect streams, compare event timing, and correlate retained audio at multiple times. | Preserve authorized source audio with correct timestamps or recreate the requested events; apply the full track once when joining parts. | Decode the entire export; test alignment near the start, middle, end, and joins. State whether audio was retained or recreated. |
| R14 | Low-resolution output is presented as Full HD | Inspect render-buffer dimensions and encoded stream metadata; inspect text and contour detail. | Render original 3D geometry at the requested native resolution, rather than enlarging an earlier preview. | Verify both canvas dimensions and FFprobe results, including the preview when final-quality review was requested. |
| R15 | Preview is too short, inaccessible, or described as the complete film | Measure its real duration/frame count and verify the requested hosted destination. | Export the requested preview length and publish it where authorized; keep preview and final delivery clearly identified. | Verify remote commit/file, duration, dimensions, and playback/decode. A local path or an active process is not publication. |
| R16 | Final delivery is incomplete or in the wrong folders | Compare delivered paths, source files, export duration, and requested naming/layout. | Put the runnable project and complete movie in the agreed folders; place previews/diagnostics outside the final-only directory. | Confirm only the intended full movie is in the final-video directory; verify complete frame count, decode, and remote commit. |

## Additional preventive checks

These are general engineering checks, not claims that every item was a user-reported defect in the original production.

| ID | Failure class | Required handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | Nondeterministic frames or state left over from another shot | Evaluate identical timestamps repeatedly and out of order; reset transient expressions, props, effects, visibility, and attachments from explicit timeline state. Compare resulting frames/transforms. |
| P02 | Optimization changes shadows, depth, outlines, occlusion, or composition | Compare equivalent frames before/after optimization, including transparent and overlapping objects. Reject incorrect caching even when it is faster. |
| P03 | Camera crosses geometry, clips a subject, or unexpectedly changes framing | Check near/far planes, camera paths, target continuity, subject bounds, and aspect ratio through the full shot. Validate from the rendered camera, not only an editor view. |
| P04 | Flicker, z-fighting, shadow artifacts, or unstable contours | Inspect animated frames and close depth overlaps; correct coplanar surfaces, normals, shadow settings, outline thickness, or sampling. Recheck temporal stability. |
| P05 | Part joins drop/repeat frames or introduce audio gaps | Assemble by exact frame boundaries, verify total count and timestamps, inspect both sides of joins, and decode the entire result. Do not use rounded audio-packet durations as video boundaries. |
| P06 | Encoder/browser fails silently or leaves a partial file reported as final | Propagate process failures and early pipe closure; verify completion, full decode, and frame count before publication. Retain logs and label incomplete files. |
| P07 | Render settings or source changes invalidate already rendered intervals | Record source/settings hashes and affected time ranges. Rerender invalid intervals at native resolution; preserve unaffected valid output only with documented equivalence. |
| P08 | Upload/commit success is assumed | Verify the remote branch SHA and intended file paths after push. Do not invent a public URL or claim availability from a local commit alone. |

## Apply to each new video

1. Copy `templates/review-ledger.json` into the project. Determine applicability per failure class and per shot/action. A missing chair makes the seating check inapplicable; it does not make all contact checks inapplicable.
2. Set project-specific tolerances using scene scale, source evidence, final resolution, and the chosen physical/stylized behavior. Do not reuse a previous film's dimensions or timestamps as universal constants.
3. Record reproducing times/ranges, affected actors/props, expected versus observed behavior, root cause, correction, and evidence. Track user observations even if they arrive while rendering.
4. Add a meaningful automated regression when a measurable invariant or a previously observed bug warrants it. Otherwise retain explicit visual review evidence. A checklist entry is not an executed test.
5. Classify every applicable check as pending, failed, or passed. Use not-applicable only with a concrete reason. Never mark a case passed merely because the template implements a related feature.
6. Reopen failures when shared rig, scene scale, camera, material, timing, or export settings change. Rerender and republish affected output. Continue independent authorized work while fixes are made.
7. Before declaring completion, resolve applicable failures and disclose any remaining limitation against the request. Do not silently replace requested behavior with a simpler method or hide unsupported fidelity behind a passing technical report.
